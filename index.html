Problem

One of the largest regional banks in Mexico needed to deliver more value to their SMB clients. Replace batch reporting with an AI-powered, always-on financial insights experience that gave small business owners real-time visibility into their finances.

Problem

Hussmann (Panasonic) needed to capture, graph, and report operational efficiency of test lab results for their commercial refrigerators. Manual processes were slow and error-prone. The goal: standardize operations and replace manual reporting with automated, live data analysis.

Approach

Discovery was challenging: client processes were not standardized. After standardizing workflows, we connected the software directly to lab machinery through LabView, built a Desktop app to capture and upload data, and a Web app for users to interact with live reports and dashboards.

Impact
  • Standardized client process end-to-end
  • 84% reduction in report time: 2 weeks → 2 days
  • Doubled lab capacity through live data analysis
  • 100% manual errors eliminated
